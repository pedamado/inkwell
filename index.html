Your hand draws: the webcam follows your index finger, finger poses set the line, the thumb calls the agents — for caregivers and able-bodied participants.

Two windows, one drawing

Open a second window of this browser with another variant and move it to the other screen — e.g. 18a for the person drawing with the eyes, 18d for the carer drawing with the hand. The windows join by themselves (no network); each draws in its own colour and uses its own camera.
